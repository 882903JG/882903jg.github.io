LIMBO

Introduction

Limbo is a private server for old ROBLOX clients, where you can play versions from their most popular years. It was made possible through archives of the old clients and server software, also from reverse engineering their software and figuring out how to patch them to point towards my servers.

Skills Development

This project has taught me a lot of skills; it gave me a good understanding of how to reverse engineer software and was very helpful in helping me learn PHP, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, as well as learning how to manage databases. For a brief period of time, Limbo was also public, which gave me experience in managing a large community, and that experience gave me skills I could use elsewhere.
