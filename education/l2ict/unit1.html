UNIT ONE THE ONLINE WORLD

Introduction

This unit covers how online systems and technologies function, such as looking deeper than what we see at the surface, learning the multiple types of technologies used that let us browse the web. It also covers the changes the internet brings to our lives, brought by the easy and fast communication, smart devices automating tasks, shopping without even leaving the house, etc. Also covered in this unit are the privacy and security concerns these technologies bring, leading us to research how to reduce security risks, and how to enhance privacy.

Skills Development

This unit led to improve my pre-existing knowledge on this area of technology, as it contained further research into the inner workings and general greater understanding of the internet and the web, and similar subjects.
