UNIT TWENTY BUILD A PC

Introduction

This unit covers the different uses of computers, and detailing the types and costs of components they could/should contain, its purpose is to teach how to create plans for and build a PC, and to follow health and safety throughout, it also details on what components should be used in different scenarios, such as a system that needs high performance components for games, or something that needs to be cheap and efficient would use low end components.

Skills Development

This unit has been helpful in developing my knowledge on health and safety during the build process, and how to choose the right components under heavy constraints, and it has also been helpful in improving my writing to explain in more detail and in justification of my reasons.
